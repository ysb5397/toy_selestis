# Stamp kit concept v1

생성 방식: 내장 image_gen. 게임 적용 전 검토용 불투명 배경 시안.

파일: stamp-kit-concept-v1.png

구성: 승인·반려 인장, 나무 도장 도구의 기본·눌림 상태. 제작 목표의 픽셀 크기와 일치하는 최종 스프라이트 시트가 아니며, 적용 전 개별 에셋 분리·실제 저해상도 크기·투명 배경·상태별 실루엣 정합 검수가 필요하다. 현재 게임 코드는 변경하지 않았다.

최종 생성 프롬프트:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a clear ART DIRECTION DRAFT BOARD for a Korean pixel-art Java-learning game titled "코드 심사국". OPAQUE background, not transparent. Entire background is warm aged cream paper #e4dfc7, flat color, no gradients, no atmospheric lighting.
A simple 2-column by 3-row arrangement of SIX isolated objects with ample spacing. No headings or captions.
TOP LEFT: large dark forest teal ink stamp IMPRESSION: double rectangular OUTLINE and big bold Korean lettering exactly "승인" inside; interior is cream paper, no solid color fill. TOP RIGHT: dark brick-red ink impression: rectangular outline with all four corners cut diagonally, big bold Korean lettering exactly "반려"; interior is cream paper. These TWO Korean words must be very legible and dark against the cream background. Subtle missing-ink pixel flecks, not fuzzy blur.
MIDDLE LEFT: chunky wooden rubber stamp tool for approval, dark walnut wood handle, dark olive iron base, small dark teal identification plate with cream Korean lettering "승인". MIDDLE RIGHT: matching tool with brick-red identification plate and cream Korean lettering "반려". Whole tools visible, straight front view. Both look like original 1990s adventure-game inventory sprites, NOT real product photos.
BOTTOM LEFT: same approval wooden tool in pressed state, shorter rubber pad. BOTTOM RIGHT: same rejection tool in pressed state. Keep all tools aligned, identical perspective and visual scale.
STYLE: bold, crisp, visibly chunky square pixel clusters; low-resolution pixel-art game sprites enlarged with nearest-neighbor look. Hard pixel edges and minimal three-tone shading. Rich walnut brown, desaturated olive-black outlines, muted teal and brick-red ink. No smooth 3D, no blur, no glow, no lighting haze, no gradients, no shadows across the board, no checkerboard, no hands, no documents beyond the solid cream backdrop, no English text, no other objects. All six objects should be distinct and plainly readable on the LIGHT cream background.
